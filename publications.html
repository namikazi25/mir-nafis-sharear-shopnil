MERIT: Modular Framework for Multimodal Misinformation Detection with Web-Grounded Reasoning

Mir Nafis Sharear Shopnil, Sharad Duwal, Abhishek Tyagi, Adiba Mahbub Proma

An inference-time framework for multimodal misinformation detection with four modules: visual forensics, cross-modal alignment, retrieval-augmented claim verification, and calibrated judgment. It produces citation-linked rationales for human review.
